# Methodology — Nonprofit Sector Needs, Coping Strategies, and Evidence of What Works

**Research window:** mid-2025 → mid-2026 (core). Earlier data used only as labeled baseline for trend comparison.
**Compiled:** July 2026

## Research design

Six parallel workstreams, each run by an independent agent with no visibility into the others' findings, so that agreement between workstreams is genuine triangulation rather than shared assumption:

| # | Workstream | Scope | Depth budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier-1 survey extraction | Needs, funding disruption, capacity crisis, compliance pressure | Standard |
| 2 | Grant-seeking economics | Application volumes, win rates, hours/cost per application, competition trend | **Deepest** |
| 3 | AI adoption paradox | Reconciled adoption stats, high-impact minority, failure modes, governance, trust | Deep |
| 4 | Practitioner voice | Reddit, LinkedIn, conferences, podcasts — the unofficial record | Standard |
| 5 | Response catalog | Nonprofit coping strategies + funder-side responses | Deep |
| 6 | Outcomes & disconfirmation | Documented results vs. announcements; adversarial testing of core claims | **Deepest** |

## Evidence grading scheme

Every claimed outcome in this research carries one of four grades. The grade is the single most important piece of metadata in the report.

- **[Documented]** — a measured result with a stated method: before/after data, an evaluation, a longitudinal panel, a controlled comparison.
- **[Reported]** — self-reported in a survey. Includes "we saved time using AI." Directionally useful; not a measurement.
- **[Announced]** — an intention, commitment, launch, or pledge with no follow-up evidence. **Never presented as working.**
- **[Anecdote]** — a single practitioner account. Signal, not prevalence.

The gap between what is [Announced] and what is [Documented] is treated as a finding in its own right, not as a gap in the research.

## Sourcing rules applied

1. **Primary over secondary.** Statistics appearing in listicles or news coverage were traced to the originating report and cited with page/section. Untraceable statistics were discarded rather than repeated.
2. **Metadata on every number.** Source, sample size, field dates, and sponsor. Vendor-sponsored surveys are flagged as directionally useful but biased toward optimism about the vendor's category.
3. **Conflicts triangulated, not resolved by preference.** Where sources disagree, the range is presented with the methodological reason for the spread — most commonly differences in question wording.
4. **Retrieval honesty.** Sources are marked as successfully fetched versus seen only as a search-result snippet. Paywalled or unfetchable Tier-1 documents are logged as such rather than substituted with third-party coverage.
5. **Disconfirmation is budgeted, not incidental.** Each workstream spent dedicated search cycles seeking evidence against its own most thesis-friendly conclusion. Workstream 6 exists primarily to attack the project's core claims.
6. **Absence of data is reported.** Where a question could not be answered, the report carries an explicit "no data found after N searches" entry. A documented gap is a result.

## Known limitations

Recorded here rather than buried, and expanded in each workstream's own data-gaps section:

- Practitioner platforms (Reddit, LinkedIn) resist automated retrieval; coverage there is opportunistic and skews toward what search engines surface.
- Several Tier-1 reports are paywalled or gated behind registration; where full text was unavailable this is logged in the source log.
- Grant-seeking economics is genuinely data-poor as a field. Gaps in that section reflect the state of the evidence, not the depth of the search.
- Vendor-sponsored AI research dominates the available adoption data, which biases the aggregate picture toward higher adoption and more favorable impact than independent research would likely find.

## Separation of evidence and interpretation

The implications appendix — what these findings mean for a funder-fit product and for responsible-AI consulting positioning — is kept in a separate section at the end, and deliberately did not shape evidence collection. Findings that argue against the commissioning thesis were explicitly solicited and appear in the main body, not only in the appendix.
