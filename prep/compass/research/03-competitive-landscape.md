# Competitive/Peer Landscape: Compass Working Capital (researched 2026-08-06)

## 1. Direct FSS-space peers

**The FSS market.** ~700 PHAs and property owners administer FSS, serving ~74,000 families; FY2025 funding $141M (flat YoY); reaches only ~3% of eligible households out of ~4.4M HUD-assisted families (Bipartisan Policy Center, CBPP). Eligibility extended to project-based rental assistance owners in 2015 (statutory via 2018 Economic Growth Act; first HUD multifamily FSS grants 2023) (Cityscape Vol.27 No.1).

**Who else operates:**
- In-house PHA programs — default model: one FSS coordinator per PHA, case-management/referral orientation, low graduation rates. Compass's graduation rate ~3x national average (3 of 4 enrolled families who remain in subsidized housing graduate).
- Nan McKay & Associates — for-profit training/consulting; 3-day FSS certification; the incumbent trainer, not an operator or scaler. NAHRO offers competing 2-day certification. HUD Exchange free FSS training.
- Compass FSS Link — Compass's own TA/field-building arm (2019): online platform, 2,400+ practitioner members; free accounts + paid customized TA and multifamily FSS launch consulting. **No other nonprofit scales FSS nationally as both direct administrator and intermediary — Compass is the category creator and sole national intermediary.** (High confidence.)

**Compass differentiation (know cold):**
- Direct administrator for 5,000+ households since 2010; $19M+ escrow accumulated; graduates average ~$10,000 escrow.
- Model = FSS escrow + dedicated personal financial coach (financial-capability orientation vs. referral FSS).
- **Evidence base (moat):** Abt Associates quasi-experimental evaluations since 2014 — Lynn/Cambridge: +$6,305 avg household earnings vs comparison; 2021: income $4,997 higher; FICO gains (BHA: 66% raised credit within 6 months, avg +30 pts); reduced public-benefit receipt; positive cost-benefit. (abtglobal.com/CompassFSS)
- **Project-based Section 8 innovation:** 2016 POAH partnership among first owner-run FSS; first-ever evaluation of owner-run FSS (2021). Partners: Boston, Cambridge, Lynn, Philadelphia housing authorities; POAH, Mercy Housing, Related, WinnCompanies, The Caleb Group, Metro Housing Boston.
- Growth from 4 states (2019) to 15 states + DC after Blue Meridian scaling investment.
- **MDRC tension (must be fluent):** MDRC's national RCT of conventional FSS (18 PHAs, ~2,600 households) found NO significant earnings/employment impacts — HUD published final results 2025-26. Shelterforce (2026-03) and Urban Institute argue national null doesn't reflect strong programs like Compass's. The "national null vs. Compass positive quasi-experimental" tension is the core argument for Compass's model and for scaling its practices (incl. via technology).

## 2. Adjacent asset-building / financial-capability nonprofits

- **Prosperity Now** (fka CFED): policy/research/field network; Scorecard; ~$20.4M revenue (2023 990). No notable AI.
- **SaverLife** (fka EARN, renamed 2019): digital savings platform, 650,000+ members. **Most advanced AI peer:** AI "Navigator" conversational financial guidance (funded GitLab Foundation, JPMC, Goodman); lessons: members value judgment-free AI, deliberately not human-named, transparency about data use.
- **UpTogether** (fka Family Independence Initiative): unrestricted cash + community platform; $210M+ to 200,000+ households since 2020; Gates grantee (Nov 2024 ~$1.06M).
- **LIFT**: 1:1 coaching for parents + $150/quarter cash (2 yrs); Chicago, LA, NY, DC. Claims $63K net benefit per family. No public AI found.
- **MyPath**: youth banking/saving/credit in youth employment programs; 90+ partners. No notable AI.
- **Working Credit NFP**: credit-building as employer benefit; Urban Institute-evaluated. No notable AI.
- **Neighborhood Trust Financial Partners**: TrustPlus workplace financial coaching; 12,000+ workers via 200+ customers in 45 states. No public LLM deployment found.
- **Change Machine** (fka The Financial Clinic): SaaS financial-coaching platform (Salesforce-based), 800+ orgs; **AI recommends vetted fintech products from customer data** — closest coach-copilot vendor analog.
- **Springboard To Opportunities**: resident services in subsidized housing + Magnolia Mother's Trust guaranteed income ($1,000/mo x 12 to Black mothers; 6 cohorts, 550+ moms). Nearest "same population, different mechanism" peer.
- **CFE Fund / Financial Empowerment Centers**: municipal free 1:1 counseling; Bloomberg-backed; toward 50 cities. No notable AI.

## 3. AI adoption among peer nonprofits

**Cautionary — NEDA "Tessa" (2023):** helpline replaced 6 unionized staff + ~200 volunteers (~70,000 calls/yr) with chatbot; vendor added generative capabilities; gave dieting advice to eating-disorder users; offline within days. Canonical citation for "strengthen, not replace."

**Cautionary — Benefits Data Trust (closed Aug 2024):** 20-year-old benefits-access nonprofit; despite $20M from MacKenzie Scott, collapsed after tech/AI team spending grew to ~40% of expenses while contract revenue never covered delivery cost. The field's warning about AI ambition outrunning the business model.

**Successes / deployments:**
- SaverLife Navigator — most on-point: AI layered on trusted content (own + CFPB), anti-anthropomorphization, data transparency.
- Robin Hood AI Poverty Challenge winners (2024-25, $1M each): Beyond 12 (gen-AI college success coaching — flagship "AI augments coaches" model), Legal Aid Society NYC (AI housing-helpline), CodePath (AI CS tutoring).
- Change Machine — AI fintech-recommendation inside coach-facing platform.
- Benchmarks: 2025 AI Equity Project: 65% interested, only 9% feel ready to adopt responsibly, 76% lack AI strategy.
- Consumer demand: Financial Health Network — gen-AI chatbot use for financial advice doubled 3%→7% (2024→2025); 43% distrust AI for financial advice vs 12% trust. FHN Responsible AI workshop Dec 2025. LISC has published on responsible AI in financial coaching.

## 5. Why an AI EIR now — logic chain

1. **Money is moving.** NextLadder ($1B), Google.org ($50M+), OpenAI ($100M), Robin Hood ($4M) all explicitly fund AI for economic mobility — Ballmer already funds Compass. EIR positions Compass to capture this capital and satisfy Blue Meridian-style scale expectations.
2. **Compass's binding constraint is coach labor.** Differentiation = dedicated coach per family; scaling nationally requires coach leverage: coach copilot (session prep, notes, escrow calc, action plans), KM across FSS Link's 2,400+ practitioners (turning TA into product), participant-facing between-session support, back-office automation of escrow/recert admin.
3. **Playbook and anti-playbook exist.** Playbook: SaverLife Navigator, Beyond 12, Change Machine, Legal Aid Society. Anti-playbook: NEDA Tessa, Benefits Data Trust. "Strengthen, not replace" is the documented failure boundary, not just values language.
4. **Evidence discipline is the brand.** Abt evaluations vs MDRC null; any AI deployment will be expected to be evaluated with same rigor — could become the first rigorously evaluated AI-augmented financial coaching model in subsidized housing (a fundable first).
5. **Policy tailwind.** Multifamily FSS is young (first grants 2023), FSS reaches 3% of eligible; HUD 2025 Cityscape symposium centers Compass's model — AI-enabled scale is the plausible answer to reaching the other 97%.

**Know cold:** FSS mechanics (escrow math, PIH notices, graduation rules, 2015/2018 multifamily expansion, FY25 $141M); Abt vs MDRC debate; Compass stats ($6,305 earnings gain, +30 pts FICO, ~$10K avg graduate escrow, 3x graduation rate, $19M escrow, 5,000+ households, 15 states + DC); funder map; SaverLife/Change Machine comparables; NEDA/BDT failures; sector readiness (9% ready, 76% no strategy; 43% distrust AI financial advice); equity frame as design constraint.
