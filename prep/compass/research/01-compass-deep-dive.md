## 2. The Compass FSS model

**Mechanics**: FSS (est. 1990) turns rent formula into savings engine — earnings rise → ~30%-of-income rent rises → increase deposited into interest-bearing escrow, received unrestricted at graduation (~5 years). Compass co-administers with PHAs/owners: housing partner runs escrow accounting; Compass replaces referral case management with client-driven financial coaching (credit, debt, goal planning) + aspirational marketing.

**Partners**: Boston HA, Cambridge HA, Lynn Housing Authority & Neighborhood Development, Metro Housing|Boston, Philadelphia HA, Chicago HA (2025), POAH, WinnCompanies, Related Companies, Mercy Housing, The Caleb Group. BHA (since 2019): 1,600+ families, $12M+ escrow; BHA FSS now 3rd largest in country.

**Cumulative impact**: 2010–2024: 5,000+ households, $19M+ escrow; graduates avg ~$10,000 escrow; 90% of Compass graduates leave with escrow vs 23% nationally (HUD 2022); graduation ~3 in 4 of families remaining in subsidized housing — ~3x national avg.

**FSS Link**: launched 2019; online learning platform + community for FSS practitioners; 2,400+ practitioners across nearly every state; premium tier + light-touch TA. Client recruitment site: joinfss.org.

**Evaluations (Abt — quasi-experimental, not RCTs)**:
- 2017 (Lynn & Cambridge): positive earnings/income effects.
- Credit & debt: scores rose for 65%, avg +39 points; 80% decreased/kept zero collections; avg collections-debt reduction $2,865; avg intake score 624.
- 2021 longitudinal + CBA (Cambridge/Boston): net societal benefit $6,999/participant over 7 yrs (variant $3,885); earnings gains ~$6,300 vs matched peers.
- 2021 POAH multifamily — first-ever evaluation of owner-run FSS: +$1,933 annual income (+17%) after 2.5 yrs.
- HUD Cityscape Vol. 27 No. 1 (2025) — special FSS issue with chapters on Compass research and its financial capability model.

## 3. Recent news & policy (2024–2026)

- Mar 2024: bipartisan Helping More Families Save Act introduced (Sens. Reed D-RI & Britt R-AL; Reps. Torres, Timmons) — nationwide opt-out FSS pilot, auto-enrolling up to 5,000 households.
- June 9, 2025: Chicago expansion — GreenLight $600K; Chicago Housing Authority; goal 1,000 families. CHA program "Build Your Nest" (June 2025) = largest test of automatic (opt-out) FSS enrollment to date; ~7 months in, ZERO families opted out, 884 contacted, 128 coaching appointments completed.
- Federal funding whiplash: FSS $141M FY2025; Trump FY2026 budget request ELIMINATED FSS funding (despite Trump signing permanent reauthorization 2018); House/Senate restored $125M/$156M; FY2027 request again eliminates FSS, Jobs-Plus, ROSS amid proposed 13% HUD cut. FY25 grant rounds funded all renewals but NO new FSS programs.
- HUD's national FSS RCT (18 PHAs, 2,656 households): no significant employment/earnings impacts, only 20% graduation; Shelterforce (Mar 12, 2026) pushed back using Compass as counterexample. Expect defense-of-the-evidence framing to be live.
- Apr 7, 2026: Compass Capital Club launched with fintech Stackwell (Black-wealth-focused robo-investing): 75 current/former FSS clients get investment accounts, workshops, up to $2,000 seed capital each — first step into public capital markets.
- June 24, 2026 — signature policy win: Helping More Families Save Act passed inside bipartisan 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act — HUD selects up to 25 PHAs to auto-enroll up to 5,000 families in opt-out FSS demonstration (10-yr sunset, report to Congress). Strategic plan centerpiece becoming law.
- July 20, 2026: with POAH, first-of-its-kind savings program for LIHTC communities in Chicago — 3-yr privately funded pilot (Chicago Community Trust), 2–3 properties, ~160 households: $250 seed, $50/mo for on-time rent, +$200/yr consistency bonus, +$400 for 2 coaching sessions — up to $1,200/yr. Extends asset model beyond Section 8 into LIHTC where no FSS statute exists.

Automating intake, scheduling, documentation, outreach is the only way coaching scales. FSS Link (2,400+ practitioners) is an obvious surface for AI-powered TA/knowledge products.

## 5. Voice and values

- Mission language (verbatim): "end asset poverty for families with low incomes"; "narrow the racial and gender wealth divides." Focus on families led by Black and/or Latinx women. Three-part theory: (1) client-centered savings + coaching, (2) training/TA (FSS Link), (3) policy solutions that dismantle barriers.
- Framing: "families with low incomes" (people-first, never "the poor"); poverty as wealth/asset problem rooted in structural racism, not personal failing; "partner with families"/families as experts in their own lives; aspirational marketing leading with dreams (homeownership, education, business); narrative-shifting is an explicit CEO priority.
- Coaching model: client-driven (client sets goals; coach guides), non-judgmental, relationship-first — documented by Abt qualitative research and Cityscape chapter as a defined "financial capability model."
- Public cumulative framing: ~6,000 people served, $20M+ collective savings; graduates avg $10K escrow; 75% hold $400+ additional personal savings.
