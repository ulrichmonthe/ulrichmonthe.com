02 · The organization

Compass Working Capital, cold

Founded 2005 in Boston by Sherry Riva, inspired by Michael Sherraden's Assets and the Poor — the thesis that poverty is a wealth problem, not just an income problem. Riva led it 17 years, hand-picked her successor, and still sits on the board (now also senior advisor to the Massachusetts Attorney General).

The model in one breath

HUD's Family Self-Sufficiency program (est. 1990) turns the rent formula into a savings engine: when a family in subsidized housing earns more, its ~30%-of-income rent rises — and FSS deposits that increase into an interest-bearing escrow account the family keeps, unrestricted, at graduation (~5 years). Compass co-administers FSS with housing authorities and multifamily owners: the housing partner runs escrow accounting; Compass replaces referral-style case management with client-driven financial coaching — credit, debt, goal-based planning — plus aspirational marketing that leads with families' dreams, not deficits.

08 · Your point of view

No-fly zones and the governance spine

When she asks for your point of view on responsible AI at Compass, this is the developed answer — specific to their programs, their data, and their clients.

No-fly zones (say these unprompted)

  • No direct financial advice or eligibility decisions to clients from generative AI. Escrow math, recertification, benefits questions — deterministic systems and humans only.

  • No client PII in any tool without consent architecture — disclosed at intake, revocable, no service penalty, readable, in Spanish.

  • No EIV-derived income data in LLMs, period. Federal restriction; PHA agreements govern everything adjacent.

  • No unreviewed funder-facing output. Human owns voice, judgment, and every number. Never fabricate outcomes.

  • Augment coach prep and follow-up; never substitute the conversation. The coach-client hour is the product.

The lightweight governance kit

  • A 2–4 page policy, not a binder: approved-tool list, data classification with red lines, human-review-before-external rule, disclosure norms for clients and funders, one named owner, small cross-functional review group, quarterly refresh. NIST AI RMF as scaffolding, worn lightly.

  • Eval gates before scale: your gold-standard non-fabrication framework and LLM-as-judge pipelines — models must cite sources and explain structural cause, or refuse. This travels from BWDC with no baggage.

  • Vendor clauses: change control (Tessa), no-training commitments, data retention, exit portability. The consultant's deliverables are subject to the same gates.

  • Monitor live outputs, not just pilots. Every public failure in this sector happened after launch, in production, unwatched.
