The Compass business model — how everybody fits

Compass is an operator that sits between four groups: government (which funds the rails), philanthropy (which funds the operator), housing partners (which hold the money mechanics), and families (who pay nothing and keep everything). Two more loops — evidence and field-building — turn one program into a national model.

Walkthrough — what each party gives and gets

The numbers match the badges on the diagram. The model's elegance: every party's incentive points the same direction — toward the family's savings account.

1

Government — HUD & Congress

Gives — housing subsidies (the rails), FSS coordinator grants, and the statute itself; the June 2026 opt-out law is the newest gift.

Gets — a rare anti-poverty program with bipartisan appeal and (via Compass) actual results to point to. FSS costs almost nothing: the escrow is the family's own foregone rent increase.

Tension — the FY26 and FY27 budget requests both tried to zero out FSS funding. Washington is simultaneously Compass's platform and its biggest single risk.

2

Philanthropy & impact capital

Gives — ~78% of Compass's revenue: Blue Meridian (up to $30M unrestricted), Ballmer ($6M), Wells Fargo ($3.45M), JPMC, GreenLight, Chicago Community Trust.

Gets — an evidence-backed, scalable lever on the racial and gender wealth gap. Funders here buy system change (opt-out law, field adoption), not just direct service.

3

Housing partners — PHAs & owners

Give — access to residents, the rent formula, escrow accounting, and service contracts (part of Compass's ~19% earned revenue).

Get — a high-performing FSS program without building coaching capacity themselves, HUD compliance, and resident success stories. Boston Housing Authority's Compass-run FSS is now the 3rd largest in the nation.

4

Compass — the operator

Gives — the three engines: coaching (direct service), FSS Link training (field-building), and policy design. Plus the enablers: ~30 coaches, Salesforce/Agentforce, Abt partnership.

Gets — philanthropic grants + partner contracts + FSS Link fees. Note the FY24 surplus ($17.6M in vs $9.1M out): multi-year commitments banked to fund the strategic plan — including this AI role.

5

Families — the client who pays nothing

Give — only participation and trust. The program is free; the savings are their own foregone rent increases returned to them.

Get — a coach, a credit-and-debt turnaround (+39 pts, $2,865 collections cleared), and an average $8,500–$10,000 unrestricted at graduation. 90% women, 95% people of color, ~$25K starting income.

6

The wider FSS field

Gives — FSS Link membership and TA fees; a national audience.

Gets — Compass's playbook. This is how Compass reaches beyond its 50+ sites toward the 97% of eligible families it will never serve directly. No other nonprofit plays both operator and national intermediary.

7

The evidence engine — Abt Global

Gives — independent rigor: quasi-experimental studies since 2014.

Gets / produces — the moat. Evidence is what converts outcomes into philanthropy (2) and policy wins (1), and what defends the model against HUD's null national RCT. Nothing at Compass scales without surviving this loop.

8

New frontiers — de-risking the statute

What it is — Stackwell (investing + seed capital for graduates), the POAH LIHTC savings pilot (escrow logic with no federal statute needed), Merit America (workforce).

Why it matters — if Washington ever kills FSS funding, these prove the asset-building model can run on private rails. "Bold, layered strategies" is Markita's own phrase for this.

The flywheel, in one sentence for the interview: government funds the rails, philanthropy funds the operator, the family's own earnings growth funds the savings — and outcomes become evidence, evidence unlocks funders and policy, policy expands the field, and the field multiplies outcomes. The one constraint every loop shares is coach capacity — roughly 30 coaches at ~400 appointments a year — which is exactly why an AI Executive in Residence exists: every hour AI returns to a coach compounds through the entire flywheel.
