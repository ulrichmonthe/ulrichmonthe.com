EIN Due-Diligence Checker

Live · v1
What One plain-English trust card for any US nonprofit: 501(c)(3) status, tax-deductibility, revocation history and filing recency, with a green / amber / red verdict.
Why Verifying an organization means reconciling three separate IRS files that routinely disagree. Most people skip it, or pay for a database that hands back raw records and leaves the judgement to you.
How Search a name or EIN. Read the verdict, expand any field to see its source and as of date. Free, no login.

Funder Standing

In build · preview
What How often a private foundation funds an organization it has not funded before, computed from its own Form 990-PF grant schedules.
Why A foundation can keep an open application portal for a decade and still fund the same thirty organizations every year. The application checkbox is self-reported and never audited. The grant ledger is behaviour.
How Search or filter the list, open a row for the evidence behind the figure, add rows to a screen and export it. Where names cannot be matched reliably, it shows no figure and says which condition failed rather than guessing.
Who Grant writers deciding where the hours go Development directors defending a pipeline to a board Consultants qualifying prospects across clients
Form 990-PF Part XVClustering, not lookupMatch rate on every row39 correctness checks

Status

  1. Pipeline built to spec — entity clustering, drift guards, publication gate, 39 checks passing
  2. IRS extraction live — 594,053 Form 990-PF filings indexed, 118 foundations in the preview cohort
  3. Now: widening the cohort, and hand-checking a sample against the filings
  4. Then: per-foundation pages and an index of how often foundations fund anyone new
