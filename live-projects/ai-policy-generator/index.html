Nonprofit AI Policy Generator

Live · v1

Answer a short form — about three minutes — and leave with a board-ready AI policy tailored to your organization: your subsector, your data, the rules that apply to you. Plus a board memo, an adoption resolution, and a staff one-pager. Free. The policy itself never asks for your email.

Your answers stay in your browser. The form, the analysis, and the policy are generated entirely on this page — nothing you type is sent to a server unless you optionally request the board packet by email. This tool provides general information, not legal advice; have counsel review before adoption where possible.
